Fluorescent cells
Source: https://imagej.net/ij/images/FluorescentCells.zip
Description: https://imagej.net/ij/images/FluorescentCells.txt
Molecular Probes demo slide of bovine pulmonary artery endothelial cells.
Blue: DAPI-stained nuclei. Green: tubulin labeled with Bodipy FL goat
anti-mouse IgG. Red: F-actin labeled with Texas Red X-Phalloidin.
The original R/G/B planes are packed into one RGB TIFF without changing
pixel values. No analysis image resizing. The embedded caption is retained
in the original; the segmentation demo analyzes only rows y=0..469.

Fruit-fly brain
Source: https://imagej.net/ij/images/flybrain.zip
Description: https://imagej.net/ij/images/flybrain.txt
Courtesy Benjamin Schmid; prepared/scanned in the Heisenberg lab,
Wuerzburg, Germany. Confocal Drosophila brain, green nc82 antibody staining,
red GAL4 driver line 78y expression. Upstream documentation says this
sample was smoothed and resampled four times per dimension before release.
All 57 RGB slices and XY calibration from that supplied sample are retained.
The mobile demo is a 2D maximum-intensity projection, not a 3D rendering.

Embryos and leaf
https://imagej.net/ij/images/embryos.jpg
https://imagej.net/ij/images/leaf.jpg
ImageJ Open Samples. The sample listing does not identify their creators.
Decoded from the original JPEG files at full size and stored in losslessly
compressed TIFF. Their embedded scale bar/ruler remains in the image;
no physical calibration is inferred from these markings.

All thumbnails are reduced previews of the supplied images. Analysis uses
the full-resolution bundled TIFF data. Source files, original description
files, preparation script, hashes and transformations accompany the source.
These image credits are separate from the new Android wrapper's GPL notice;
the port does not claim authorship or assign a new license to sample images.
